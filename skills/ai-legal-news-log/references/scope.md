# Scope Reference — AI Legal News

This file expands the scope summary in SKILL.md with category-specific examples, key actors/dockets to watch, research queries, and the US primary + lead-generator source list.

---

## Core daily watch list

### 1. IP — Copyright

- Training-data fair-use litigation: NYT v. OpenAI, Bartz v. Anthropic, Kadrey v. Meta, Getty v. Stability, music-label suits against Suno/Udio, Concord v. Anthropic (lyrics)
- DMCA §1202 CMI-removal theories
- Copyright Office AI guidance and registration denials
- Fair-use rulings on model training and output
- Music rights, voice-clone publicity, image-generation output liability

**Research queries:** `training data fair use ruling`, `NYT OpenAI ruling`, `Copyright Office AI registration`, `DMCA 1202 AI training`, `AI generated work copyright`

### 2. IP — Patent, PTAB, USPTO

- §101 eligibility for ML and AI claims (Federal Circuit)
- USPTO AI inventorship guidance, Thaler/DABUS progeny
- PTAB IPR decisions on AI/ML patents
- Patent prosecution guidance for AI claim drafting
- USPTO use-of-AI-in-practice rules for practitioners

**Research queries:** `Federal Circuit AI patent 101`, `USPTO AI guidance notice`, `PTAB IPR machine learning decision`, `USPTO AI inventorship`, `Federal Register USPTO AI`

### 3. IP — Other (Trademark, Trade Secrets, Publicity)

- Trade-secret protection of model weights, training data, prompts
- ELVIS Act (TN), NO FAKES Act (federal), state right-of-publicity laws on AI voice/image clones
- Trademark disputes over AI brand names

### 4. Privacy & Data Protection

- State comprehensive privacy laws with AI/ADMT provisions: CCPA ADMT regs, Colorado AI Act, Texas TRAIGA, Utah AI Policy Act, Virginia, Connecticut
- BIPA and state biometric statutes applied to face/voice models
- FTC §5 "algorithmic disgorgement" (Rite Aid, Amazon/Ring, WW/Kurbo)
- COPPA + generative AI
- Web scraping for training data

**Research queries:** `California CCPA ADMT regulation`, `Colorado AI Act enforcement`, `Texas TRAIGA AI`, `BIPA AI lawsuit`, `FTC algorithmic disgorgement`

### 5. Employment & Labor

- EEOC guidance on automated hiring
- Mobley v. Workday
- NYC Local Law 144 AEDT audits
- Illinois AI Video Interview Act
- California FEHA AI regulations
- ADA accommodation conflicts with AI screening
- Workplace surveillance and AI monitoring
- WGA/SAG-AFTRA AI contract provisions as bargaining template

**Research queries:** `EEOC AI hiring guidance`, `Mobley Workday ruling`, `NYC AEDT audit`, `California FEHA AI regulation`, `AI workplace surveillance`

### 6. Consumer Protection / AI Washing

- FTC Operation AI Comply (DoNotPay, Automators AI, Rytr, Ascend Ecom)
- SEC AI-washing enforcement (Delphia, Global Predictions, Presto)
- State AG actions (CA, NY, TX)
- Deceptive-AI claim materiality
- Algorithmic price-fixing (overlaps with antitrust)

**Research queries:** `FTC Operation AI Comply`, `SEC AI washing settlement`, `state AG AI enforcement`, `FTC deceptive AI claim`

### 7. Securities & Financial Services

- Board AI oversight and Caremark duties
- 10-K AI risk-factor disclosure trends
- SEC predictive-data-analytics rule status
- CFPB algorithmic lending, ECOA/Reg B adverse-action notices for AI denials
- FINRA AI guidance
- AML model governance

**Research queries:** `SEC AI disclosure`, `CFPB algorithmic lending`, `FINRA AI guidance`, `10-K AI risk factor`, `SEC predictive analytics rule`

### 8. AI Company Capital Markets Events

- S-1 filings from AI companies (CoreWeave, Cerebras, Scale AI, xAI, Anthropic-related, OpenAI if/when)
- IPO pricing and post-IPO 10-K first-year AI risk factor patterns
- Direct listings and SPAC mergers involving AI companies
- Secondary offerings
- Major private rounds with governance/disclosure implications (e.g., unusual board structures, export-control disclosures)

**Research queries:** `AI company S-1 filing SEC`, `AI IPO priced`, `AI company direct listing`, `AI SPAC merger`, `[company name] S-1 EDGAR`

### 9. Antitrust & Merger Review

- DOJ/FTC review of AI M&A: MSFT-OpenAI, Amazon-Anthropic, Google-Character.AI
- Section 2 / refusal-to-deal theories on model access
- Algorithmic price-fixing: RealPage, Yardi, Agri Stats
- Hiring-algorithm collusion
- NVIDIA and hyperscaler investigations
- FTC 6(b) inquiries into AI partnerships

**Research queries:** `DOJ AI antitrust investigation`, `FTC AI merger review`, `algorithmic price fixing lawsuit`, `RealPage antitrust`, `AI refusal to deal antitrust`

### 10. Healthcare

- FDA SaMD / PCCP (Predetermined Change Control Plans) framework
- State medical-board rules on clinician AI use (CA SB 1120, others)
- AI prior-authorization litigation: UHC nH Predict, Cigna PXDX
- Clinical-decision-support liability

**Research queries:** `FDA AI SaMD guidance`, `FDA PCCP approval`, `AI prior authorization lawsuit`, `state clinician AI rule`

### 11. Product Liability & Torts

- AV litigation: Tesla Autopilot/FSD
- Chatbot liability: Moffatt v. Air Canada, Garcia v. Character.AI, Raine v. OpenAI
- Section 230 and generative output
- Negligent design / failure-to-warn for LLMs

**Research queries:** `chatbot liability lawsuit`, `Tesla Autopilot lawsuit`, `Character AI lawsuit`, `Section 230 generative AI`

### 12. Commercial Contracting

- AI vendor terms: model, data, output IP ownership
- Indemnities: Microsoft Copyright Commitment, OpenAI, Google, Adobe Firefly
- Input-use restrictions and prompt confidentiality
- Model-deprecation / change-in-model clauses
- SLAs for hallucination/accuracy
- M&A diligence of AI assets and training-data provenance

**Research queries:** `AI vendor indemnity`, `Microsoft Copilot indemnification`, `AI contract terms update`, `M&A AI due diligence`

### 13. Litigation, Discovery & Evidence

- Mata v. Avianca progeny — hallucinated-citation sanctions
- Kohls v. Ellison — AI-tainted expert declarations
- Proposed FRE 707 on AI evidence / deepfakes
- Authentication under 901/902
- Discovery of training data, system prompts, RAG corpora
- Judge-specific standing orders on AI in filings (200+ and growing)
- Sedona Conference AI principles

**Research queries:** `AI hallucinated citation sanction`, `FRE 707 AI evidence`, `deepfake evidence authentication`, `AI standing order court`, `Sedona AI principles`

### 14. Legal Ethics

- ABA Formal Op. 512
- State bar opinions: CA, FL, NY, NJ, DC, TX
- Competence (1.1), confidentiality (1.6), supervision (5.1/5.3), billing (1.5)
- Fee-sharing concerns with AI vendors

**Research queries:** `state bar AI ethics opinion`, `ABA 512 AI`, `lawyer AI confidentiality rule`

### 15. Government / Federal Action

- Trump executive orders on AI (EO 14179 revoking Biden EO, Preventing Woke AI, AI Infrastructure, AI Action Plan)
- OMB memos on federal AI use
- BIS export controls on chips and frontier models
- Treasury/FinCEN AI guidance
- NIST AI RMF updates
- Federal AI procurement rules (OMB, GSA)

**Research queries:** `executive order AI`, `OMB AI memo`, `BIS AI export control`, `NIST AI RMF`, `federal AI procurement rule`

Include stories even if they fall outside the core/secondary lists when:

- **US–China AI competition** — export controls, investment screening, CFIUS actions on AI deals, tit-for-tat tech restrictions
- **Major AI infrastructure deals with legal implications** — large compute contracts, data-center deals, power-purchase agreements for AI workloads
- **Foreign AI regulation affecting US companies** — EU AI Act enforcement against a US company, UK Online Safety Act
- **Single dominant story of the day** — something that will appear on every practice-group newsletter next morning
